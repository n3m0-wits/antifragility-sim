## antifragility-sim
# Systemic Fragility and Moral Hazard: An Agent-Based Simulation of the 'Greenspan Put'
----
This project develops an Agent-Based Simulation model (ABM) of the macroscopic effects of central bank volatility supression. 

By simulating a network of utiilty-optimising central banks, the model will attempt to demonstrate how the implicit guarantee of bailouts altered institutional risk profiles. 

We will contrast an interventionist monetary policy with a low-intervention one, and attempt to show that suppressing short-term volatility through business cycle smoothing increases the likelyhood of systemic tail-risk (Black Swan) events. (See: Taleb & Blyth, 2011).
